# Dante X Product Intelligence

Dante X synthesizes useful product/decision concepts from market tools without cloning their UI or proprietary logic.

## Locked principles

- Scenario-first and confluence filtering: competing CE and PE hypotheses are built before authorization.
- Discovery is separate from authorization: radar candidates can rank highly and still remain WAIT/blocked.
- Momentum, breakout/compression and mean-reversion evidence are descriptive families, not automatic trades.
- Underlying and option-premium response must agree enough to justify an option setup.
- Statistical/positioning evidence is bounded; OI, PCR and IV are synthesized rather than double-counted.
- Risk/reward is mandatory for an actionable setup.
- Sector, breadth, multi-index and multi-timeframe context act as confirmation/counterweights.
- Price response, exhaustion, volume and liquidity matter; failed hypotheses are cut rather than defended.
- Strike Intelligence compares execution quality, Greeks, decay, OI, volume and spread.
- Evidence-aware alerts report meaningful evidence changes, not every price tick.
- Market Pulse is the compact app-facing evidence summary.
- Performance Journal tracks labelled SHADOW outcomes using R, MFE, MAE, expectancy and profit factor.

## Separation rule

Direction probability != trade quality != risk/reward != authorization.

A strong contract or attractive R:R must never inflate directional probability. Probability remains unavailable until calibrated out-of-sample evidence is sufficient.

## Safety and validation

The current system is SHADOW/read-only. No broker execution is performed. Freshness and required-source gates fail closed. Tomorrow's live session is an engineering test environment, not proof of predictive edge.
