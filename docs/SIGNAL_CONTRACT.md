# Actionable Signal Contract

Every actionable signal must contain:

- status/lifecycle
- symbol and exact contract when applicable
- side (bullish/bearish)
- horizon
- exact underlying trigger
- premium trigger when relevant
- executable entry range
- structural stop/invalidation
- quantity derived from risk budget
- T1/T2+ targets
- expected net reward/risk
- explicit cancel/flip condition
- Live Confirmation
- Path Match
- Potential Left
- evidence and conflict notes
- timestamp and expiry
- calibration state

The operator sees the verdict first. Explanations are secondary.

Intraday signals expire the same trading day. They cannot become positional merely because the trade has not worked.
