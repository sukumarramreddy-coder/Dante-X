# Adversarial execution rules

Dante X must survive hostile market paths, not just clean trends.

- A gap through a trigger does not automatically become a market entry. The setup has a pre-authorized maximum execution distance.
- An underlying breakout without option confirmation can block the option trade.
- Low Potential Left blocks a late chase even when direction remains correct.
- A breakout that immediately falls back through the relevant prior extreme is treated as failure evidence.
- Trigger then structural invalidation is failure, not permission to widen the stop.
- Cancelling one side does not authorize the opposite side. The opposite thesis must independently pass evidence, execution-quality and instrument-quality gates.
- No instant CE-to-PE or PE-to-CE revenge flip.
- Near-expiry gamma/theta conditions can tighten authorization requirements or block a setup.
- Every blocked setup remains auditable so the laboratory can determine whether the guard helped or hurt out of sample.
