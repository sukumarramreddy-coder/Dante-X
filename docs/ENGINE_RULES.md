# Engine Rules

These rules are invariants, not UI preferences.

1. **Verdict is derived, not narrated.** A candidate either fails authorization, becomes ARMED, fires GO, or is cancelled.
2. **ARMED trigger immutability.** Approaching a trigger is not a reason to move it.
3. **Price response outranks catalyst narrative.** Expected-vs-actual response can reverse the weight of a catalyst.
4. **Independent evidence only.** Correlated indicators are collapsed by family before scoring.
5. **Instrument != direction.** A directional hypothesis may be valid while a particular option is rejected for spread, liquidity, decay or poor payoff.
6. **Potential Left is mandatory.** A move that is mostly spent is not chased simply because confirmation is high.
7. **Natural invalidation first; quantity last.**
8. **Net R:R after estimated costs/slippage is mandatory.**
9. **Both sides remain alive.** Bullish and bearish hypotheses coexist until evidence changes them.
10. **Horizon is declared before entry.** Intraday does not mutate into positional.
11. **No fake probability.** Unvalidated scores remain scores.
12. **Audit everything.** State transitions, evidence, MFE/MAE, costs and outcomes are retained for later calibration.
