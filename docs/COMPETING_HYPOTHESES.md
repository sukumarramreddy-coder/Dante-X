# Competing hypotheses

Dante X never begins a session as “bullish” or “bearish.”

Bullish and bearish theses exist simultaneously and are updated from the same market observations. Each side has its own evidence, path, option behavior, potential, liquidity and execution-quality state.

A high score is not enough. Each side must independently pass executability gates.

If both sides are strong and their scores are too close, the correct state is CONFLICT / NO TRADE rather than choosing one arbitrarily.

A directional takeover requires more than the opposite score becoming numerically larger. The new side must strengthen while the old side deteriorates, and the new side must independently authorize.

An existing position never changes the evidence weights. Position management and market inference are separate layers.
